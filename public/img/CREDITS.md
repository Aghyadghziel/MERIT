# Photography credits

All photography on this site is **placeholder material from Unsplash**, used under the
[Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license). It stands in for the commissioned campaign
photography a real label would shoot, and it is the first thing that would be replaced.

The people pictured are not associated with MERIT and do not endorse it. MERIT is a fictional
label; see `/about` and `/terms`.

## Before this goes anywhere public

Photographer names are **not yet filled in below**. Unsplash's search and API were not reachable
without an API key when these were gathered, so only the canonical source URL of each image could
be verified. Two options, both fine:

1. Replace every file with commissioned or licensed photography, or
2. Look each ID up at its source URL and complete the `Photographer` column.

Nothing here should be published with the attribution column blank.

## Outfit frames

`outfits/*.svg` are drawn placeholders generated by `tools/outfits/build.mjs`, not photographs.
They exist to prove the carousel's alignment contract and are to be replaced by commissioned
photography of one model in one pose.

## Rejected

Candidates showing other labels' logos, shopfronts or branded packaging were discarded during
selection rather than cropped, along with anything whose colour could not be brought into the
bone / ink / oxide palette without distorting the photograph.
